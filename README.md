## Where the promo code box hides at checkout

The code field on armani.com isn't on the cart page — it's a collapsed link in the order summary, which is why plenty of people check out without ever seeing it.

1. Add your items and press "Add to Cart" on the product page.
2. Click "View Cart" in the panel that slides out on the right.
3. On the order summary page, click **"Do you have a promo code?"**
4. Enter the code exactly as you received it — capitalization and spacing count.
5. Press "Apply." The discount should come off your total immediately, before payment.

If the discount doesn't appear right away, don't pay and hope for the best. Codes don't apply retroactively.

Nine times out of ten it's one of these:

- **You're not logged in — or logged in with the wrong account.** Email-exclusive codes only work with the account that received them. Most codes also require a logged-in account to validate at all.
